Lopunny Dudunsparce deck guide

Mega Lopunny ex attacks for a single Energy. Its Gale Thrust does 230 on a turn it moves from your Bench to the Active Spot, and Dudunsparce draws the cards that set that up. Both of its attacks cost only Colorless Energy, so any Energy powers them.

  • Tournament record: 51.1% wins over 3,197 games since 16 Sep 2026, and 52.3% over 495 games since Delta Reign.
  • RdyDeck grade: 7.6 out of 10 for its top lists.

Game plan

Gale Thrust costs 1 Colorless Energy and does 60, plus 170 more if Mega Lopunny ex moved from your Bench to the Active Spot this turn. Retreating your Active Pokémon into Lopunny does that, and Air Balloon makes the Retreat Cost 2 Colorless less. Spiky Hopper does 160 for 2 Energy, and no effect on your opponent's Active Pokémon changes that damage.

Dunsparce evolves into Dudunsparce, whose Run Away Draw draws 3 cards and then shuffles Dudunsparce and its cards back into your deck, so the same Pokémon can draw again later. Most lists add a Dudunsparce ex, whose Tenacious Tail does 60 for each of your opponent's Pokémon ex in play. Fan Rotom's Fan Call searches for up to 3 Colorless Pokémon with 100 HP or less on your first turn.

Mist Energy prevents all effects of your opponent's attacks done to the Pokémon it is attached to, and Battle Cage stops damage counters being put on Benched Pokémon by your opponent's attacks and Abilities, which blunts attacks that spread damage counters. Wally's Compassion heals all damage from a Mega Evolution Pokémon ex and returns its Energy to your hand.

How RdyDeck grades it

RdyDeck grades the 4 Lopunny Dudunsparce lists in its test set, all top 16 finishes from 7 Nov 2026 to 9 Nov 2026, at 7.6 out of 10 (the middle value). Their middle Synergy is 8.1 and their middle Power is 7.0. The grade is not simply Synergy times Power, since it also counts the deck's online results and lowers the grade for how tightly a list fits only when the fit is loose.

RdyDeck counts Gale Thrust at its full 230 when a list has enough cards to switch Lopunny in or retreat for free. It still grades Lopunny Dudunsparce lower than most top decks, because 230 takes two hits against the format's biggest Pokémon while Mega Lopunny ex gives up 3 prizes when it is Knocked Out. Dudunsparce's draw every turn counts in its favor.

Your own list will grade differently, since the grade reads every card you run. How grading works explains each part.
