RdyDeck is a free Pokémon TCG deck grader and prize trainer run by Optume Ventures LLC, a Pennsylvania limited liability company ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information RdyDeck uses, where it goes, and the choices you have. It covers the website at rdydeck.com and the RdyDeck app, including the Google Play version.

The short version

  • There are no accounts. You never give us your name, email or password to use RdyDeck.
  • Your decks, scores, notes and settings are saved in your own browser on your own device. We do not have a copy.
  • RdyDeck is free and paid for by ads. Ads are shown by Google, which may use cookies or similar technology. What kind of ads you see depends on the age group you choose and, where the law requires it, on your consent.
  • To show card names, text and pictures, RdyDeck asks public card databases for card data. Those services see your IP address, like any website you visit.

Information saved on your device

RdyDeck saves the following in your browser's local storage and IndexedDB, on your device only:

  • Deck lists you add, deck names and cover cards
  • Prize game results, drill results, tournament log entries and matchup notes
  • Settings such as theme, cards per row and whether you have seen the tour
  • Your age group (under 13, 13 to 17, 18 or over, or no answer). We keep only the group, never your exact age or birth date.

This information is not sent to us. It stays until you delete it with Settings, Clear all data, clear your browser's site data, or uninstall the app. If you use Save and open decks, RdyDeck makes a file or text copy that you control; we never receive it.

The app also keeps a copy of its own files and of card pictures you have viewed so it works offline. That copy holds no personal information.

Ads and cookies

RdyDeck shows a small number of ads through Google AdSense, in fixed spots such as the results screen and the bottom of a deck's grade. Ads never appear during a game or drill. Google and its partners may use cookies, device identifiers and your IP address to show, measure and limit ads, and to prevent fraud. You can learn how Google uses this information at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services, and manage personalized ads at Google's ad settings.

  • Age groups. The first time ads would appear, RdyDeck asks your age. If you say you are under 13, or you skip the question, every ad request is marked for child treatment, which means no personalized ads. If you say you are 13 to 17, ad requests are marked for teen treatment. You can move to a younger group at any time in Settings.
  • Consent in the EEA, UK and Switzerland. Where the law requires it, RdyDeck shows a consent message from [NAME OF GOOGLE CERTIFIED CONSENT PLATFORM] before personalized ads load, and you can change your choice at any time from [LINK OR SETTING].
  • US state privacy rights. Personalized advertising may count as "selling" or "sharing" personal information under some US state laws. You can opt out at [DO NOT SELL OR SHARE LINK OR SETTING]. RdyDeck also honors the Global Privacy Control signal from your browser [confirm how this is set up].
  • Offline. When your device is offline, no ad loads.

Children

RdyDeck is made for players of all ages, so it treats anyone under 13, or anyone who does not give an age, as a child for ads. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. We do not ask children for any contact details, and no personal information is sent to us. The age answer and all decks stay on the device. Ads shown to children are requested as child directed, without personalization, as described above.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child's information reached us, contact us at [CONTACT EMAIL] and we will help.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete everything RdyDeck saved: Settings, Clear all data, or clear site data in your browser.
  • Turn off personalized ads: choose a younger age group, use your consent choices, or use Google's ad settings.
  • Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to the use of personal information. Because we do not hold personal information about you, most requests can be met by clearing your device. For anything else, contact us.
