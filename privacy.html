Privacy Policy

Effective November 9, 2026

RdyDeck is a free Pokémon TCG deck grader and prize trainer run by Optume Ventures LLC, a Pennsylvania limited liability company ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information RdyDeck uses, where it goes, and the choices you have. It covers RdyDeck on the web and in the RdyDeck app, including any Google Play version.

The short version

  • There are no accounts. You never give us your name, email or password to use RdyDeck.
  • Your decks, scores, notes and settings are saved in your own browser on your own device. We do not have a copy.
  • RdyDeck is free. The website pays for itself with a few ads from Google AdSense. The Google Play app shows no ads.
  • Players under 13, players who skip the age question and players 13 to 17 never get personalized ads.
  • RdyDeck uses no analytics tools.
  • To show card names, text and pictures, RdyDeck asks public card databases for card data. Those services see your IP address, like any website you visit.

Information saved on your device

RdyDeck saves the following in your browser's local storage and IndexedDB, on your device only:

  • Deck lists you add, deck names and cover cards
  • Prize game results, drill results, tournament log entries and matchup notes
  • Settings such as theme, cards per row and whether you have seen the tour
  • Your age group (under 13, 13 to 17, 18 or over, or no answer) if you answer the age question. Only the group is kept, never your age or birth date.

This information is not sent to us. It stays until you delete it with Settings, Clear all data, clear your browser's site data, or uninstall the app. If you use Save and open decks, RdyDeck makes a file or text copy that you control; we never receive it.

The app also keeps a copy of its own files and of card pictures you have viewed so it works offline. That copy holds no personal information.

Information other services receive

When RdyDeck loads, the services below receive the technical details every web request carries, such as your IP address, browser type and the time of the request. We do not receive this information from them.

Deck share links carry the deck in the part of the address after the # sign. Browsers do not send that part to any server, so sharing a deck does not send its list to us or to the host. Moving your decks from the old address to rdydeck.com works the same way.

RdyDeck does not use analytics tools. Google AdSense is the only advertising service it loads, as described below. If that ever changes, this policy will be updated first.

Feedback by email

Send feedback opens an email to us at support@rdydeck.com in your own email app. The email is filled in with your message, the kind of feedback, the RdyDeck version, the screen you were on, your device type (such as Android or iPhone) and screen width, and your current deck list only if you tick Include my list. You can change or delete any of it, and nothing is sent until you press Send. RdyDeck itself sends nothing.

If you send it, we receive your email address and what the email says. We use it only to answer you and improve RdyDeck, we do not add you to any list, and we keep it only as long as we need it for that. To have us delete your email, ask us.

Ads and cookies

The RdyDeck website shows at most one ad on a screen, on a few screens: the home screen, the results after a prize game, the bottom of a deck grade and the guide pages. It never shows ads during a game or drill, never shows pop-up or full-screen ads, and shows no ads when you are offline. The Google Play app shows no ads.

Ads are provided by Google AdSense. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the Internet. Google may also use cookies or similar technology to limit how often you see an ad, measure ads and prevent fraud.

  • You can opt out of personalized advertising in Google's Ads Settings, or opt out of some other vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising at www.aboutads.info.
  • Google explains what it does with information from sites that show its ads in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.
  • In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, Google's consent message asks for your choices before personalized ads are shown. You can change them at any time with the privacy and cookie settings link that the message adds to the site.

Younger players. Before any ad loads in the app, RdyDeck asks your age once. It does not block anyone, and only the age group is kept, on your device. The guide pages use the same answer. For players under 13, for anyone who skips the question, and on the guide pages for anyone who has not answered it, each ad request is marked for child-directed treatment, so Google does not show personalized ads or remarketing ads for it. For players 13 to 17, each ad request is marked for teen treatment, and Google does not show personalized ads for it either.

Buy links and tips. Players 13 and over may see links to buy a deck from a card store. These are affiliate links: if you buy, we may earn a small fee, and the store may know you came from RdyDeck. Players 18 and over may see a link to leave a tip on Ko-fi. These links open the other site, whose own privacy policy applies there. Players under 13 do not see them.

Children

RdyDeck is made for players of all ages. It has no accounts and does not collect personal information from anyone, including children: decks, notes and settings stay on the device. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ads shown to players under 13, or to anyone who does not answer the age question, are requested as child-directed, as described under Ads and cookies.

Players under 13 should ask a parent or guardian to send feedback for them. If we learn that a feedback email came from a child under 13, we will reply at most once and then delete it.

If you are a parent or guardian and have a question about your child's use of RdyDeck, contact us at mmmilliard@optumeventures.com and we will help.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete everything RdyDeck saved: Settings, Clear all data, or clear site data in your browser.
  • Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to the use of personal information. Because we do not hold personal information about you, most requests can be met by clearing your device. For anything else, contact us.

Security and retention

RdyDeck is served over HTTPS. Information saved on your device stays until you delete it. We do not run servers that store your information. Our host may keep short server logs under its own policy.

Google Play

If RdyDeck is released on Google Play, that version will be the same app in a secure web container, with one difference: it shows no ads and loads no ad code. Its Data safety answers will describe the same practices as this page.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top and, for important changes, show a notice in the app.
