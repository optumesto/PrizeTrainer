# Play Store graphics

- icon-512.png: 512x512, 32-bit PNG (RGBA), same artwork as icon.svg.
- feature-graphic-1024x500.png: 1024x500, logo, wordmark and tagline from the app, with the real Grade screen.
- phone-1 to phone-7: 1080x1920 portrait screenshots of real screens, using the Mega Rayquaza starter deck (no user data). Play needs 2 to 8; they are numbered in the suggested upload order.

Screenshots are 1080x1920 because Play does not accept a long side more than twice the short side (1080x2400 is too tall).
