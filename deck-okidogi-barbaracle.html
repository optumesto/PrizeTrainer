Okidogi Barbaracle Deck Guide

A Fighting deck built around Okidogi, a Basic Pokémon that becomes a 230 HP attacker doing 170 damage once it has Darkness Energy attached. Okidogi is the main attacker, and every Pokémon in the core list gives up only one Prize card, so your opponent usually needs six Knock Outs to win.

  • Tournament record: 54.6% wins over 537 games since 16 Sep 2026, and 56.6% over 198 games since Delta Reign.
  • RdyDeck grade: 9.0 out of 10 for its top lists.

Game Plan

Okidogi's Adrena-Power gives it +100 HP and makes its attacks do 100 more damage to your opponent's Active Pokémon while it has any Darkness Energy attached. Prism Energy provides every type of Energy while attached to a Basic Pokémon, one at a time, so one Prism Energy turns Adrena-Power on, and the ACE SPEC Legacy Energy does the same. With Adrena-Power on, Good Punch does 170 for two Fighting Energy.

Barbaracle's Stone Arms attaches a Basic Fighting Energy from your hand to one of your Fighting Pokémon once a turn, on top of your normal attachment. Lunatone's Lunar Cycle discards a Basic Fighting Energy from your hand to draw 3 cards while you have Solrock in play, and Solrock's Cosmic Beam does 70 for one Fighting Energy, not affected by Weakness or Resistance, as long as Lunatone is on your Bench. Fighting Gong finds a Basic Fighting Energy or a Basic Fighting Pokémon, and Poké Pad finds any Pokémon without a Rule Box.

Bloodmoon Ursaluna's Battle-Hardened attaches up to 2 Basic Fighting Energy from your hand when you bench it, and Mad Bite does 100 plus 30 for each damage counter on your opponent's Active Pokémon, which finishes big Pokémon that Okidogi has already hit. Battle Cage stops the effects of attacks and Abilities from your opponent's Pokémon from placing damage counters on Benched Pokémon, so attacks like Phantom Dive can't put damage counters on your Bench while it is in play.

How RdyDeck Grades It

RdyDeck grades the one Okidogi Barbaracle list in its test set, a top 16 finish on 8 Nov 2026, at 9.0 out of 10. Its Synergy is 7.2 and its Power is 8.1. The grade is not simply Synergy times Power, since it also counts the deck's online results and lowers the grade for how tightly a list fits only when the fit is loose.

RdyDeck expects this deck to need two hits for the biggest Pokémon in the format, with Bloodmoon Ursaluna's Mad Bite as its hardest single hit. Every Pokémon gives up one Prize card, so the trade stays even against decks of Pokémon ex. Synergy loses points for spreading the Pokémon over many lines with several single copies, and the deck's record above even since Delta Reign lifts its grade over what the cards alone earn.
