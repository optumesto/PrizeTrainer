# PrizeTrainer v18: cleaner look

Before: thick 3px borders, square corners, little corner squares, colored end cap bars on every header, hard offset shadows, a graph paper background and tiny pixel font labels made the app feel blocky.

After: the same layout, colors and features with a softer, cleaner finish.

## What changed
- Rounded corners on cards, panels, modals, buttons, inputs and prize slots.
- Borders thinned to 1px and tinted, so color still marks each section without boxing it in.
- Corner squares and header end cap bars removed.
- Hard offset shadows replaced with soft ones. Buttons now shrink slightly when pressed instead of jumping sideways.
- Graph paper grid removed from the background. The soft blue glow at the top stays.
- Buttons, tabs, small labels and card names now use Nunito at readable sizes. The pixel font stays for the title, deck names, scores, the timer and other numbers.
- Badges, pips, energy buttons and the card counter are pills.
- Prize slots are solid and rounded instead of dashed.
- Weak Spots thumbnails no longer spill broken image text.
- Light theme: the Import and Guess buttons are readable now.

## Under the hood
- All of it is one CSS layer added at the end of the style block, so nothing in the layout or scripts moved.
- Cache bumped to prize-trainer-v18 so phones pick up the new look.
- Includes every v17 fix.

## Tested
- Full suite: 26 of 26 pass (import, Cards, Grade and Odds tabs, odds math, standard, tournament and drill games, reload, offline cache, no console errors).
- v17 regression checks: 15 of 15 pass.
- No horizontal overflow at phone width. No en or em dashes in the files.

## To install
Upload index.html and sw.js from this folder over the current ones, keeping those exact names. The repo already has v17 live, and this is built on that exact file. index.txt and sw.txt in the repo are leftovers from the v16 upload and can be deleted.
