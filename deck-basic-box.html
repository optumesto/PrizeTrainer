Basic Box deck guide

Basic Box plays only Basic Pokémon and picks a different attacker every turn. With Area Zero Underdepths and a Tera Pokémon in play it can hold up to 8 Pokémon on the Bench, so the right answer is usually already waiting.

  • Field share: 6.8% of players in the 5 Limitless online events in the 14 days to 10 Nov 2026 (1,266 players).
  • Online record: 53.5% wins over 5,138 games since 16 Sep 2026, and 52.7% over 450 games since Delta Reign.
  • RdyDeck grade: 8.9 out of 10 for its top lists.

Game plan

Every Pokémon in the list is a Basic, so there is nothing to evolve and no turn spent waiting. Teal Mask Ogerpon ex's Teal Dance attaches a Basic Grass Energy from your hand to itself and draws a card, Mega Kangaskhan ex draws 2 with Run Errand from the Active Spot, and Latias ex gives every Basic Pokémon free retreat. Area Zero Underdepths lets a player with a Tera Pokémon in play, like Teal Mask Ogerpon ex, have up to 8 Pokémon on their Bench.

The attackers each solve a different problem. Raging Bolt ex's Bellowing Thunder discards any amount of Basic Energy from your Pokémon and does 70 damage for each one, so it can take one huge knockout. Iron Leaves ex's Rapid Vernier switches it into the Active Spot when you bench it and moves any amount of Energy from your other Pokémon onto it, so it can attack for 180 out of nowhere. Lillie's Clefairy ex's Fairy Zone makes your opponent's Dragon Pokémon weak to Psychic, a tool against Dragapult.

Energy Switch, Crispin and Glass Trumpet keep the Energy moving. Glass Trumpet works while you have a Tera Pokémon in play and attaches a Basic Energy from your discard pile to each of up to two Benched Colorless Pokémon, such as Mega Kangaskhan ex, which turns Energy spent on Bellowing Thunder back into fuel. The price is prizes: nearly every Pokémon gives up 2, and Mega Kangaskhan ex gives up 3.

How RdyDeck grades it

RdyDeck grades the 10 Basic Box lists in its test set, all top 16 finishes from 7 Nov 2026 to 9 Nov 2026, at 8.9 out of 10 (the middle value). Their middle Synergy is 7.9 and their middle Power is 9.1. The grade is not simply Synergy times Power, since it also counts the deck's online results and lowers the grade for how tightly a list fits only when the fit is loose.

Basic Box grades among the highest decks in RdyDeck. Power is near full because six different Pokémon in a typical list can hit for 170 or more, and its Abilities draw cards every turn. Synergy reads lower than the grade, because a toolbox spreads its cards over many single copies, but that fit check only lowers the grade when the fit is very loose.

Your own list will grade differently, since the grade reads every card you run. How grading works explains each part.
