How Standard rotation works

Most cards carry a regulation mark, a letter in the bottom corner. Once a year, the oldest legal mark leaves Standard. Recent years followed one pattern: D left in 2023, E in 2024, F in 2025 and G in 2026, each announced in January and gone from events in April.

What the checker shows

  • Not legal now: cards that cannot be played in Standard today. A deck with any of these grades 4.9 at most until they are swapped.
  • May rotate next: cards with the mark expected to leave at the next rotation.
  • Safe: cards whose mark is confirmed to stay. A card counts as safe only when its mark is confirmed.

Plan your next list around the safe cards, and use the deck grader to see how the deck holds up once the rotating cards are gone.
