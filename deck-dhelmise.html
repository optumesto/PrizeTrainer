Dhelmise Deck Guide

Dhelmise's Vengeful Anchor does 170 damage for a single Psychic Energy once you have 4 Pokémon with the Hide 'n' Sneak Ability in your discard pile. The deck fills its own discard pile on purpose.

  • Field share: 2.7% of players in the 5 Limitless online events in the 14 days to 23 Nov 2026 (1,014 players).
  • Tournament record: 50.9% wins over 5,930 games since 16 Sep 2026, and 47.0% over 768 games since Delta Reign.
  • RdyDeck grade: 8.5 out of 10 for its top lists.

Game Plan

Shuppet, Banette, Poltchageist and Sinistcha all have Hide 'n' Sneak, which prevents all effects of your opponent's attacks and Abilities on them, though damage still goes through. Gwynn discards up to 2 Pokémon without a Rule Box from your hand and draws 3 cards for each one, so one card fills the discard pile and draws up to 6. Ultra Ball and Prism Tower discard more cards along the way.

With 4 Hide 'n' Sneak Pokémon in the discard pile, Vengeful Anchor does 30 plus 140, so 170 damage. Dhelmise is a Basic Pokémon with 140 HP that gives up one prize. Sinistcha's Matcha Spin places 4 damage counters on each of your opponent's Pokémon once 6 Hide 'n' Sneak Pokémon are in your discard pile, which softens the whole board for Dhelmise. Banette's Puppet Pull does 80 and lets you search your deck for any card.

Telepathic Psychic Energy searches for up to 2 Basic Psychic Pokémon and puts them onto your Bench when you attach it from your hand to a Psychic Pokémon. Every list plays 2 Lillie's Clefairy ex, whose Fairy Zone makes your opponent's Dragon Pokémon weak to Psychic, so Vengeful Anchor does double damage to Dragapult ex. Legacy Energy, the usual ACE SPEC, makes your opponent take one fewer Prize card the first time a Pokémon with it attached is Knocked Out by an attack.

How RdyDeck Grades It

RdyDeck grades the 3 Dhelmise lists in its test set, all top 16 finishes from 8 Nov 2026 to 9 Nov 2026, at 8.5 out of 10 (the middle value). Their middle Synergy is 9.2 and their middle Power is 8.5. The grade is not simply Synergy times Power, since it also counts the deck's online results and lowers the grade for how tightly a list fits only when the fit is loose.

Synergy is high: the discard plan has every piece it needs. Power reads the deck's best hit as two hits for the biggest Pokémon ex, which still keeps the prize race even, since Dhelmise gives up one prize. Its online record is about even, which keeps the results part of the grade near the middle.

Your own list will grade differently, since the grade reads every card you run. How Grading Works explains each part.
