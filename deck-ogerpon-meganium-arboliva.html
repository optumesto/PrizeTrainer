Ogerpon Meganium Arboliva Deck Guide

Arboliva ex is the main attacker in this Grass deck. Teal Mask Ogerpon ex and Meganium are its Energy engine: Teal Dance attaches a Basic Grass Energy every turn, and Wild Growth makes each Basic Grass Energy provide two Grass Energy.

  • Tournament record: 56.2% wins over 1,427 games since 16 Sep 2026, and 54.4% over 259 games since Delta Reign.
  • RdyDeck grade: 9.4 out of 10 for its top lists.

Game Plan

Arboliva ex is a 310 HP Stage 2 Pokémon that evolves from Dolliv and Smoliv. Oil Salvo costs one Grass Energy and does 20 damage six times to your opponent's Pokémon, split any way you like, and that damage is not affected by Weakness or Resistance, so it can finish damaged Pokémon anywhere on their board. Aroma Shot does 160 and recovers Arboliva ex from all Special Conditions. It costs three Colorless, so with Meganium in play two Basic Grass Energy pay for it.

Teal Mask Ogerpon ex and Meganium power it. Teal Dance attaches a Basic Grass Energy from your hand to Ogerpon once a turn and draws a card, Meganium's Wild Growth makes every Basic Grass Energy on your Pokémon provide two Grass Energy, and Energy Switch moves a Basic Energy onto Arboliva ex. Ogerpon is a backup attacker: Myriad Leaf Shower does 30 plus 30 for each Energy attached to both Active Pokémon, and Wild Growth's extra Energy counts for it. Dolliv's Nutrients heals 40 damage from one of your Pokémon.

Meganium and Arboliva ex are both Stage 2 Pokémon and the list plays no Rare Candy. Forest of Vitality lets Grass Pokémon evolve during the turn they are played, except on a player's first turn, and Dawn searches your deck for a Basic Pokémon, a Stage 1 Pokémon and a Stage 2 Pokémon. Bug Catching Set takes Grass Pokémon and Basic Grass Energy from the top 7 cards, Meowth ex's Last-Ditch Catch finds a Supporter when you bench it, and with Briar you take one more Prize card when Teal Mask Ogerpon ex, a Tera Pokémon, knocks out your opponent's Active Pokémon while they have exactly 2 Prize cards left.

How RdyDeck Grades It

RdyDeck grades the 3 Ogerpon Meganium Arboliva lists in its test set, all top 16 finishes from 8 Nov 2026 to 9 Nov 2026, at 9.4 out of 10 (the middle value). Their middle Synergy is 8.2 and their middle Power is 8.9. The grade is not simply Synergy times Power, since it also counts the deck's online results and lowers the grade for how tightly a list fits only when the fit is loose.

Setup speed is where this deck loses points: Meganium and Arboliva ex both evolve the long way without Rare Candy, so RdyDeck expects Arboliva ex to attack from your third turn. Synergy loses a little for the number of single copies, and the deck's record above even over the last two sets lifts its grade.

Your own list will grade differently, since the grade reads every card you run. How Grading Works explains each part.
