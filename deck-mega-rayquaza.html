Mega Rayquaza deck guide

Mega Rayquaza ex spreads Fire and Lightning Energy across a board of Basic Pokémon ex, then Storm Emerald does 50 damage for every one of them. It is the most played deck in recent online tournaments.

  • Field share: 12.9% of players in the 5 Limitless online events in the 14 days to 10 Nov 2026 (1,266 players).
  • Online record: 49.0% wins over 726 games since Delta Reign came out.
  • RdyDeck grade: 7.8 out of 10 for its top lists.
Grade my Mega Rayquaza list

Game plan

Storm Emerald costs Fire, Lightning and Colorless and does 50 damage for each Fire Energy and each Lightning Energy attached to all of your Pokémon, not only to Mega Rayquaza ex. Every Energy you attach anywhere on your board adds 50. Six in play is 300 damage and seven is 350, enough for nearly any Pokémon in the format.

Most of the deck exists to get that Energy into play quickly. Ethan's Ho-Oh ex's Golden Flame attaches up to two Basic Fire Energy from your hand to one of your Benched Ethan's Pokémon once a turn. Mega Rayquaza ex's Ruler's Roar attaches a Basic Energy from the top 4 cards of your deck to itself when you play it onto your Bench. Crispin finds two Basic Energy of different types, puts one in your hand and attaches the other. Energy Switch then moves a Basic Energy to whichever Pokémon is about to attack.

Mega Kangaskhan ex draws 2 cards with Run Errand while it sits in the Active Spot, and Latias ex's Skyliner gives your Basic Pokémon no Retreat Cost, so you can draw with Kangaskhan and then retreat into Mega Rayquaza ex for free. Talonflame ex puts itself onto your Bench from your hand with Excited Dive while you have a Colorless Mega Evolution Pokémon ex in play, and its Talon Hunt searches your deck for any 2 cards. Legendary Pyroduct turns off the Abilities of Evolution Pokémon in play for both players, which shuts down engines like Drakloak and Meganium while your Basic Pokémon keep theirs.

The cost is prizes. Every Pokémon in the list is a Pokémon ex, and the two Mega Evolution Pokémon ex give up 3 prizes each when they are Knocked Out. The deck has to win the race with big hits rather than trade evenly.

Key cards

Mega Rayquaza ex
The attacker. Storm Emerald does 50 for each Fire and Lightning Energy on all your Pokémon, and Ruler's Roar gives it a free Energy when you bench it.
Ethan's Ho-Oh ex
Golden Flame attaches two Basic Fire Energy at a time, the fastest way to grow Storm Emerald.
Mega Kangaskhan ex
Run Errand draws 2 a turn from the Active Spot. Its own Rapid-Fire Combo is a backup attack for three Colorless Energy.
Latias ex
Skyliner gives every Basic Pokémon free retreat, so you can draw in the Active Spot and still attack with Rayquaza.
Legendary Pyroduct
A Stadium that turns off Evolution Pokémon's Abilities, which hurts most other top decks more than this one.
Energy Switch
Four copies move Basic Energy onto the attacker, so the Energy count is never wasted.

How RdyDeck grades it

RdyDeck grades the 15 Mega Rayquaza lists in its test set, all top 16 finishes from 7 Nov 2026 to 9 Nov 2026, at 7.8 out of 10 (the middle value). Their middle Synergy is 8.0 and their middle Power is 8.1. The grade is not simply Synergy times Power, since it also counts the deck's online results and lowers the grade for how tightly a list fits only when the fit is loose.

RdyDeck grades winning Mega Rayquaza lists lower than most other top decks. Power reads Storm Emerald as a two hit plan for the biggest Pokémon early in a game, since the full 350 needs seven Energy in play, and every Pokémon in the list gives up 2 or 3 prizes, which costs it on the prize trade. Its online record since Delta Reign is close to even, so the results part of the grade sits near the middle too.

Your own list will grade differently, since the grade reads every card you run. How grading works explains each part.
