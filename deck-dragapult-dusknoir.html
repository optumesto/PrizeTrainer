Dragapult Dusknoir deck guide

A Dragapult ex build that adds Dusknoir. Dusclops and Dusknoir can Knock themselves Out to put 5 or 13 damage counters on any of your opponent's Pokémon, finishing what Phantom Dive starts.

  • Online record: 50.0% wins over 4,147 games since 16 Sep 2026, and 54.0% over 291 games since Delta Reign.
  • RdyDeck grade: 8.5 out of 10 for its top lists.
Grade my Dragapult Dusknoir list

Game plan

The core is the same as Dragapult: Dreepy, Drakloak and Dragapult ex, with Buddy-Buddy Poffin to bench Dreepy and Drakloak's Recon Directive to draw on the way up. Phantom Dive does 200 and puts 6 damage counters on your opponent's Benched Pokémon in any way you like, and Dragapult ex takes no damage from attacks while it is on your Bench.

Duskull evolves into Dusclops and then Dusknoir. Dusclops's Cursed Blast puts 5 damage counters, and Dusknoir's puts 13, on any 1 of your opponent's Pokémon, and then Knocks Out the Pokémon that used it. That gives your opponent a Prize card, so use it when the Knock Out it sets up is worth more. Duskull's Come and Get You puts up to 3 Duskull from your discard pile back onto your Bench, so the line can be used again.

Budew's Itchy Pollen costs no Energy and stops your opponent from playing Items during their next turn, which buys time while the Stage 2 Pokémon come online. Meowth ex's Last-Ditch Catch finds a Supporter when you put it onto your Bench, and Fezandipiti ex's Flip the Script draws 3 cards on your turn after your opponent Knocked Out one of your Pokémon.

How RdyDeck grades it

RdyDeck grades the 3 Dragapult Dusknoir lists in its test set, all top 16 finishes from 8 Nov 2026 to 9 Nov 2026, at 8.5 out of 10 (the middle value). Their middle Synergy is 8.4 and their middle Power is 8.9. The grade is not simply Synergy times Power, since it also counts the deck's online results and lowers the grade for how tightly a list fits only when the fit is loose.

Its cards grade about the same as the main Dragapult build's. Its online record is close to even, below the main build's, so its grade comes out a little lower.
