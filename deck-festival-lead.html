Festival Lead Deck Guide

Festival Lead decks attack twice a turn. With Festival Grounds in play, Dipplin, Goldeen and Seaking may each use an attack twice, and if the first one Knocks Out the Active Pokémon, the second hits the new one.

  • Tournament record: 52.5% wins over 3,140 games since 16 Sep 2026, and 51.6% over 587 games since Delta Reign.
  • RdyDeck grade: 8.1 out of 10 for its top lists.
Grade my Festival Lead list

Game Plan

Dipplin's Do the Wave costs one Grass Energy and does 20 damage for each of your Benched Pokémon, so a full Bench of 5 makes it 100, twice a turn. Buddy-Buddy Poffin and Poké Pad fill the Bench fast, and Festival Grounds has to stay in play for Festival Lead to work, which is why lists run 3 or 4 of it.

Grookey evolves into Thwackey, the engine. Boom Boom Groove searches your deck for any card once a turn while your Active Pokémon has the Festival Lead Ability, and each Thwackey in play can use its own. Seaking's Rapid Draw does 60 and draws 2 cards, and with Festival Lead it does both twice. Rellor evolves into Rabsca, whose Spherical Shield prevents all damage and effects of your opponent's attacks on your Benched Pokémon.

Every bonus to an attack counts twice. Brave Bangle adds 30 damage to each attack against an Active Pokémon ex from a Pokémon without a Rule Box, and Gladion's Final Battle, played as the last card in your hand, adds 80 to each attack that turn. Festival Grounds also keeps any Pokémon with Energy attached, on both sides, free of Special Conditions.

How RdyDeck Grades It

RdyDeck grades the 3 Festival Lead lists in its test set, all top 16 finishes from 8 Nov 2026 to 9 Nov 2026, at 8.1 out of 10 (the middle value). Their middle Synergy is 8.4 and their middle Power is 8.9. The grade is not simply Synergy times Power, since it also counts the deck's online results and lowers the grade for how tightly a list fits only when the fit is loose.

Power reads close to the top decks, since two attacks a turn with Brave Bangle or Gladion's Final Battle add up fast. Synergy varies more from list to list, because the deck runs two Stage 1 lines and has to keep Festival Grounds in play. Its online record is a little above even.

Your own list will grade differently, since the grade reads every card you run. How Grading Works explains each part.
