How to play
1
Import your deckPaste your Limitless TCG 60 card list and the app builds your card gallery automatically.
2
Flip through the visible cardsSwipe left and right. Some cards are secretly set aside as prizes before you start.
3
Guess every prize cardType your guesses with autocomplete, then tap Reveal prizes.
4
Challenge modesTap Modes on a deck to change the prize count (4, 6 or 8), add a time limit, or both.
5
Track weak spotsAfter 3 sessions the app shows which cards you miss most often, so you know what to focus on.
6
Practice openings and share listsOpen a deck and use Practice, then Hand, to drill turn 1 lines. The link button at the top gives you a link that opens the list in Prize Trainer.
7
Goldfish, drills and notesAll three live under the Practice tab of a deck. Hand can goldfish your first three turns. Drills has resource counts and an opponent prize read. Notes keeps matchup plans, an edit log of every card change, and a compare view. Card art you have seen is saved for offline play.
