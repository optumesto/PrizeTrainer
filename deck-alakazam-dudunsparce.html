Alakazam Dudunsparce deck guide

Alakazam's Powerful Hand places 2 damage counters on your opponent's Active Pokémon for each card in your hand. This deck draws as many cards as it can, and the more it holds, the harder it hits.

  • Online record: 51.7% wins over 4,937 games since 16 Sep 2026, and 51.2% over 637 games since Delta Reign.
  • RdyDeck grade: 8.2 out of 10 for its top lists.

Game plan

Abra evolves into Kadabra and then Alakazam, and both evolutions draw when you play them from your hand to evolve: Kadabra's Psychic Draw draws 2 and Alakazam's draws 3. Rare Candy skips Kadabra when you need Alakazam fast, and Dawn searches your deck for a Basic, a Stage 1 and a Stage 2 Pokémon at once. Dudunsparce's Run Away Draw draws 3 more and then shuffles Dudunsparce and its attached cards back into your deck, so you can play it again later.

Powerful Hand costs a single Psychic Energy. With 11 cards in hand it places 22 damage counters, the same as 220 damage. Enriching Energy, the usual ACE SPEC, draws 4 cards when you attach it from your hand. Because Powerful Hand places damage counters rather than doing damage, Weakness and Resistance don't apply to it.

Alakazam gives up just one prize, so the deck can trade one Pokémon for each two prize Pokémon ex the other side loses. Battle Cage stops damage counters from being placed on Benched Pokémon by the opponent's attacks and Abilities, which protects your Abra and Kadabra from spread like Phantom Dive's.

How RdyDeck grades it

RdyDeck grades the 4 Alakazam Dudunsparce lists in its test set, all top 16 finishes from 8 Nov 2026 to 9 Nov 2026, at 8.2 out of 10 (the middle value). Their middle Synergy is 9.8 and their middle Power is 8.2. The grade is not simply Synergy times Power, since it also counts the deck's online results and lowers the grade for how tightly a list fits only when the fit is loose.

Synergy is close to perfect for this deck: every evolution has its line and the deck draws every turn. Power holds it back a little. RdyDeck estimates Powerful Hand at about 220 from an 11 card hand, which means two hits for the biggest Pokémon ex, though each Alakazam gives up only one prize.
