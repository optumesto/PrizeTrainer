Dragapult Dudunsparce deck guide

A Dragapult ex build that adds two kinds of Dudunsparce: the regular one to draw cards and Dudunsparce ex as a second attacker. It has the best online win rate of any deck in RdyDeck's data over the last two sets, from fewer games than the main Dragapult build.

  • Field share: 3.1% of players in the 5 Limitless online events in the 14 days to 16 Nov 2026 (1,022 players).
  • Online record: 56.1% wins over 1,510 games since 16 Sep 2026, and 57.7% over 662 games since Delta Reign.
  • RdyDeck grade: 9.0 out of 10 for its top lists.
Grade my Dragapult Dudunsparce list

Game plan

The core is the same as Dragapult: Dreepy, Drakloak and Dragapult ex, with Buddy-Buddy Poffin to bench Dreepy and Drakloak's Recon Directive to draw on the way up. Phantom Dive does 200 and puts 6 damage counters on your opponent's Benched Pokémon in any way you like, and Munkidori's Adrena-Brain moves damage counters from your Pokémon to theirs.

Dudunsparce's Run Away Draw draws 3 cards and then shuffles Dudunsparce back into your deck. Dudunsparce ex evolves from the same Dunsparce and attacks instead: Tenacious Tail does 60 damage for each of your opponent's Pokémon ex in play for a single Colorless Energy, which hits very hard against decks built from Pokémon ex like Mega Rayquaza or Basic Box. Its Destructive Drill does 150 and is not affected by any effects on your opponent's Active Pokémon.

Risky Ruins places 2 damage counters on any Basic Pokémon that is not a Darkness Pokémon when a player puts it onto their Bench during their turn. It hits both players, but it adds to Phantom Dive's spread against decks that bench lots of Basic Pokémon. Poké Pad searches for any Pokémon without a Rule Box, like Dreepy, Drakloak or Dunsparce.

How RdyDeck grades it

RdyDeck grades the 7 Dragapult Dudunsparce lists in its test set, all top 16 finishes from 7 Nov 2026 to 9 Nov 2026, at 9.0 out of 10 (the middle value). Their middle Synergy is 8.6 and their middle Power is 8.8. The grade is not simply Synergy times Power, since it also counts the deck's online results and lowers the grade for how tightly a list fits only when the fit is loose.

RdyDeck grades this the highest of the top decks. Its cards grade the same way as Dragapult's, with spread counted above the printed 200 and Drakloak keeping Setup speed high, and its online record is the best in the data, which lifts the grade further. The record comes from fewer games than the main build, so the results part counts for a little less.
