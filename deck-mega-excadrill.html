Mega Excadrill deck guide

Mega Excadrill ex hits for 330 once it has two extra Energy attached. Metang's Metal Maker attaches Metal Energy from the top of your deck every turn, so the deck can power it up fast.

  • Field share: 3.6% of players in the 5 Limitless online events in the 14 days to 16 Nov 2026 (1,022 players).
  • Online record: 50.8% wins over 4,164 games since 16 Sep 2026, and 52.5% over 512 games since Delta Reign.
  • RdyDeck grade: 8.7 out of 10 for its top lists.

Game plan

Metang's Metal Maker looks at the top 4 cards of your deck once a turn and attaches any Basic Metal Energy there to your Pokémon in any way you like, and each Metang in play can use its own. With about 16 Metal Energy in the deck, a few Metang can load an attacker in one turn. Genesect ex's Metallic Signal searches your deck for up to 2 Evolution Metal Pokémon, like Metang and Mega Excadrill ex.

Drilbur's Call for Family puts up to 2 Basic Pokémon onto your Bench, then Drilbur evolves into Mega Excadrill ex. Maximum Drilling costs 3 Metal Energy and does 200, or 330 if it has at least 2 more Energy attached, enough for most Pokémon ex in one hit. Mega Excadrill ex has 340 HP, and Jumbo Ice Cream heals 80 from it once it has 3 or more Energy. It gives up 3 Prize cards when Knocked Out.

Metang also evolves into Metagross, the second big attacker: Metallic Hammer does 150, or 300 if you discard 3 Metal Energy from it. Energy Recycler shuffles up to 5 Basic Energy from your discard pile back into your deck, where Metal Maker can find them again. Team Rocket's Petrel searches for any Trainer you need.

How RdyDeck grades it

RdyDeck grades the 4 Mega Excadrill lists in its test set, all top 16 finishes from 8 Nov 2026 to 9 Nov 2026, at 8.7 out of 10 (the middle value). Their middle Synergy is 7.6 and their middle Power is 9.2. The grade is not simply Synergy times Power, since it also counts the deck's online results and lowers the grade for how tightly a list fits only when the fit is loose.

Power is among the highest of any deck RdyDeck grades, since Maximum Drilling Knocks Out most Pokémon in one hit. Synergy reads lower and varies more between lists. Its online record is close to even.
