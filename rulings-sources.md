## Known gap

The rulebook cited (Twilight Masquerade, 2024) is the newest English edition that could be read on Nov 16, 2026. TPCi says a newer rulebook exists (February 2026, Ascended Heroes design), but no public link to it was found, and pokemon.com/us/pokemon-tcg/rules refused automated reads. When the current PDF link is known, recheck every page number in rulings.json. Rules the February update changed are cited from the rbu26 post, not the rulebook.
