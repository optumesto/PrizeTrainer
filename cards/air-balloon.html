Air Balloon

Card text

The Retreat Cost of the Pokémon this card is attached to is ColorlessColorless less.

You may attach any number of Pokémon Tools to your Pokémon during your turn. You may attach only 1 Pokémon Tool to each Pokémon, and it stays attached.

In plain words

Air Balloon is a Pokémon Tool that lowers the Retreat Cost of the Pokémon holding it by two Colorless Energy. A Pokémon with a Retreat Cost of two or less can then retreat for free, so you can swap Pokémon without spending Energy. Six of the decks RdyDeck tracks play it in most of their top lists.

Each Pokémon can hold only one Pokémon Tool, so Air Balloon takes the place of any other Tool on that Pokémon, and it stays attached. Toucannon usually runs four, Seaking Festival Lead three, and Lucario Hariyama, Mega Greninja and Mega Chandelure two.

Official rules for it

How many Pokémon Tools can a Pokémon have?
One at a time. You can play as many Tools in a turn as you like. Source: Pokémon TCG Rulebook, The Pokémon Company International (Twilight Masquerade edition, 2024, pp. 12 and 41).
How often can you retreat?
Once per turn. Discard Energy from the Active Pokémon equal to its Retreat Cost, then switch it with a Benched Pokémon. You can still attack that turn. Source: Pokémon TCG Rulebook, The Pokémon Company International (Twilight Masquerade edition, 2024, p. 12).

When a rule and a card's text disagree, the card's text wins, and a judge at your event has the final word.
