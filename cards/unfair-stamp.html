Card guide

Unfair Stamp

Unfair Stamp is an ACE SPEC Item card from Twilight Masquerade (TWM 165), regulation mark H. It is played most in Dragapult, in 95% of that deck's top lists, and 22 other decks run it too.

Grade a deck with Unfair Stamp

Card text

You can use this card only if any of your Pokémon were Knocked Out during your opponent's last turn. Each player shuffles their hand into their deck. Then, you draw 5 cards, and your opponent draws 2 cards.

You may play any number of Item cards during your turn.

ACE SPEC: You can't have more than 1 ACE SPEC card in your deck.

Official rules for it

How many ACE SPEC cards can a deck have?
One. A deck can hold only 1 ACE SPEC card in total, not 1 of each. Source: Pokémon TCG Rulebook, The Pokémon Company International (Twilight Masquerade edition, 2024, p. 23).

When a rule and a card's text disagree, the card's text wins, and a judge at your event has the final word.
