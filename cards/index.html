Guides to the Pokémon TCG cards that top decks play right now: each card's text, the decks that run it and how often, what it does in those decks, and the official rules that apply to it.

A card is here when at least half of the top lists of a guided deck, or of one of the 20 most played decks, run it. Numbers are from Limitless online tournaments from 16 Sep 2026 to 15 Nov 2026.
