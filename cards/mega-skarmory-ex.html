In plain words

Mega Skarmory ex is a Basic Metal Pokémon with 260 HP. Its attack, Sonic Ripper, does 220 damage to any one of your opponent's Pokémon, including a Benched one, and then shuffles all Energy on Mega Skarmory ex back into your deck.

Decks play one copy to reach a Pokémon on the Bench that would otherwise be hard to hit. Since it loses its Energy after attacking, it needs a fresh three Energy each time. The Mega Excadrill and Steven's Metagross decks each play it in most top lists. It has no Retreat Cost, and it gives up 3 Prize cards when Knocked Out.

Official rules for it

Do Weakness and Resistance count when an attack hits the Bench?
No. Weakness and Resistance only count for the Active Pokémon. Damage to a Benched Pokémon ignores both. Source: Pokémon TCG Rulebook, The Pokémon Company International (Twilight Masquerade edition, 2024, p. 14).
How many Prize cards is a Pokémon ex worth?
When a Pokémon ex is Knocked Out, its owner's opponent takes 2 Prize cards instead of 1. This rulebook edition came out before Mega Evolution Pokémon ex, which carry their own rule on the card: 3 Prize cards. Source: Pokémon TCG Rulebook, The Pokémon Company International (Twilight Masquerade edition, 2024, p. 24).

When a rule and a card's text disagree, the card's text wins, and a judge at your event has the final word.
