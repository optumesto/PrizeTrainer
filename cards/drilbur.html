Official rules for it

When a card says "up to" a number, can you choose 0?
For an attack, yes: you can choose any number from 0 to the most it allows. For other effects, like Trainer cards and Abilities, you must choose at least 1, unless nothing can be chosen. An effect that searches for "any" card, without naming a kind, also needs at least 1. Source: February 2026 Rule Book Updates, The Pokémon Company International (Pokémon Professor Community post, 26 Feb 2026, "Up to" change).

When a rule and a card's text disagree, the card's text wins, and a judge at your event has the final word.
