Genesect ex

In Plain Words

Genesect ex is a Basic Metal Pokémon ex with 220 HP. Its Metallic Signal Ability searches your deck for up to 2 Evolution Metal Pokémon once a turn. Mega Excadrill and Steven's Metagross play 2 copies in every top list to find their evolutions, and Cinccino plays it in 95% of lists.

It only finds Evolution Pokémon of the Metal type, so it can't grab Basics. Its attack, Protect Charge, does 150 for two Metal and one Colorless Energy and cuts the damage it takes next turn by 30. As a Pokémon ex, it gives up two Prize cards when Knocked Out.

Official Rules for It

In what order is an attack's damage worked out?
Start from the damage printed on the attack. Apply effects on the attacking Pokémon, then add Weakness, then take away Resistance, then apply effects on the Pokémon being hit. Put 1 damage counter on it for each 10 damage left. Source: Pokémon TCG Rulebook, The Pokémon Company International (Twilight Masquerade edition, 2024, p. 20).
What if the damage works out to 0?
If the damage ends at 0 or less, no damage counters are put on the Pokémon. If the attack's own damage is 0, the other damage steps are skipped. Source: Pokémon TCG Rulebook, The Pokémon Company International (Twilight Masquerade edition, 2024, p. 20).
How many Prize cards is a Pokémon ex worth?
When a Pokémon ex is Knocked Out, its owner's opponent takes 2 Prize cards instead of 1. This rulebook edition came out before Mega Evolution Pokémon ex, which carry their own rule on the card: 3 Prize cards. Source: Pokémon TCG Rulebook, The Pokémon Company International (Twilight Masquerade edition, 2024, p. 24).
Can you use an Ability from the Bench?
Yes. Abilities work from the Active Spot or the Bench, and you can still attack after using one. Source: Pokémon TCG Rulebook, The Pokémon Company International (Twilight Masquerade edition, 2024, pp. 12 and 40).

When a rule and a card's text disagree, the card's text wins, and a judge at your event has the final word.
