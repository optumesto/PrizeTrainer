Cynthia's Garchomp ex

In Plain Words

Cynthia's Garchomp ex is a Stage 2 Pokémon ex and the main attacker of the Cynthia's Garchomp deck, which runs 3 in every top list. Draconic Buster does 260 damage for two Fighting Energy, but you discard all Energy from it afterward.

Corkscrew Dive costs one Fighting Energy, does 100 and lets you draw until you have 6 cards in hand. It has 330 HP and no Retreat Cost, and Cynthia's Power Weight can raise that HP by 70. As a Pokémon ex it gives up 2 Prize cards when Knocked Out.

Official Rules for It

How many Prize cards is a Pokémon ex worth?
When a Pokémon ex is Knocked Out, its owner's opponent takes 2 Prize cards instead of 1. This rulebook edition came out before Mega Evolution Pokémon ex, which carry their own rule on the card: 3 Prize cards. Source: Pokémon TCG Rulebook, The Pokémon Company International (Twilight Masquerade edition, 2024, p. 24).
When can a Pokémon evolve?
Not on the turn it came into play, and not on either player's own first turn, unless a card says otherwise. The same Pokémon cannot evolve twice in one turn. Evolving keeps its damage counters and attached cards, and ends Special Conditions and effects of attacks on it. Source: Pokémon TCG Rulebook, The Pokémon Company International (Twilight Masquerade edition, 2024, p. 11).

When a rule and a card's text disagree, the card's text wins, and a judge at your event has the final word.
