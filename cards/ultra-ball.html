Ultra Ball

Card Text

You can use this card only if you discard 2 other cards from your hand. Search your deck for a Pokémon, reveal it, and put it into your hand. Then, shuffle your deck.

You may play any number of Item cards during your turn.

In Plain Words

Ultra Ball is an Item that searches your deck for any Pokémon and puts it into your hand. To play it, you discard 2 other cards from your hand. Since it finds any Pokémon, it can get a Basic, an Evolution card or the attacker you need.

The 2-card discard is the price, so you need 2 other cards in hand to play it. Most decks that play it run 4: Dragapult Blaziken, Dragapult Dusknoir, Sharpedo Toxtricity, Lucario Hariyama and Mega Rayquaza usually play 4 in nearly every top list, and Dragapult usually plays 3.
