Questions about RdyDeck

Answers to the questions players ask most about RdyDeck. If yours is not here, the contact page says how to reach us.

The basics

What is RdyDeck?
RdyDeck is a free web app for Pokémon Trading Card Game players. It rates a 60 card deck out of 10 and says what to fix first, shows your opening odds, checks which cards rotate out of Standard, helps you build a deck from what top players run, and trains you to work out your prize cards.
Does RdyDeck grade card condition?
No. RdyDeck rates how well a deck plays, from the cards in its list. It does not grade the condition of a physical card the way card grading services do. Its grade out of 10 is about the deck: do the cards work together, and can the plan win.
Is it really free?
Yes. Every feature is free, there is no paid tier and nothing is locked. There are no ads yet. The website is planned to be paid for by a few ads, never by a subscription.
Do I need an account?
No. There is no sign up and no log in. Your decks, scores and settings are saved in your own browser on your own device.
Does it work on my phone?
Yes. RdyDeck runs in any modern browser on a phone, tablet or computer. You can also install it to your home screen from the browser menu, and it then opens like an app.
Does it work offline?
Once it has loaded, yes. Grading, odds, the prize trainer and your saved decks all work with no signal. Card pictures you have not viewed before need a connection the first time.
Why does part of RdyDeck not load on school or work wifi?
Some school, work and other managed networks block outside sites, including the card databases RdyDeck reads card data from. When a network blocks them, RdyDeck now loads card data through rdydeck.com instead. If something still will not load, try another network or mobile data.
How do I make my Pokémon deck better?
Paste it into RdyDeck. The grade comes with findings that name the cards to change first, such as an attacker that cannot pay for its attack or a missing draw engine. Fix the top one, grade again and watch the number move. How to improve your deck walks through the same checks by hand.
Which languages does RdyDeck support?
English, Spanish, Portuguese, French, German and Italian. Pick one under Settings. Card names and card text stay in English, so they match Pokémon TCG Live exports and Limitless lists.
Does RdyDeck work for Pokémon TCG Pocket?
No. RdyDeck grades 60 card decks for the Pokémon Trading Card Game, played with paper cards or in Pokémon TCG Live. Pokémon TCG Pocket decks have 20 cards and different rules.
Is RdyDeck made by The Pokémon Company?
No. RdyDeck is an unofficial fan-made app by Optume Ventures LLC. It is not affiliated with Nintendo, Creatures, GAME FREAK or The Pokémon Company.

Adding your deck

Which deck lists can I import?
Paste an export from Pokémon TCG Live or a list from Limitless TCG: one card per line with the count, name, set code and number. Section headings like Pokémon, Trainer and Energy are fine to leave in.
How do I get my list out of Pokémon TCG Live?
In Pokémon TCG Live, open the deck and use its export option, which copies the list. Then paste it into RdyDeck with Import a deck on the home screen.
Can I try it without a list?
Yes. Starter decks are built in, so you can see a grade before you paste your own list.
Can I share a deck?
Yes. A share link carries the whole deck inside the link itself, so whoever opens it sees the same list. The list is never uploaded to us.
Can I move my decks to another device?
Yes, one deck at a time with a share link: open the link on the other device and save the deck there. There are no accounts, so decks do not sync between your devices on their own.

The grade

How is the grade worked out?
The grade is Synergy times Power, divided by 10. Synergy checks that your cards support each other, and Power checks that the plan can win. When your deck matches a deck from recent online and local in-person tournaments, part of the grade also comes from that deck's results. How grading works explains every part.
Why did my deck get a low grade?
Look at the findings under the grade, which name the cards involved. The usual causes are an attacker that cannot pay for its attack, an evolution with nothing to evolve from, no steady way to draw cards, or a card that is not Standard legal, which holds the grade at 4.9 until it is swapped out.
Can a deck get a 10?
Rarely. Strong decks often give up a little on purpose, such as a tech card that is dead in some matchups. Most top 16 lists from recent online events grade between 7.8 and 8.9, and the highest of the 132 we test with grades 9.0.
Why does a top deck not grade higher than mine?
The grade reads cards and results, not who played them. A list can win an event and still have a gap the grader sees, and a list can grade well and lose to a bad matchup. Treat the grade as a strong opinion about the list, not a prediction of one event.
The grade seems wrong for my deck. What should I do?
Read the findings first, since they say what the grader thinks each card does. If one is wrong, use Send feedback in the app. Card roles are read from card text, and reports from players are how mistakes get found and fixed.
Where do the tournament numbers come from?
From Limitless TCG online tournaments and local in-person store events. RdyDeck reads them once a week and credits them on every screen that uses them. The deck guides show the numbers for each top deck.

Rotation and legality

How does the rotation check work?
RdyDeck reads each card's regulation mark and shows which cards are not Standard legal now, which may rotate next, and which are safe. The rotation checker page explains it in full.
My card is an old printing. Is it legal?
RdyDeck counts an older printing as legal when a reprint with the same name, attacks and Abilities is legal now, which follows the official reprint rule. So old art is fine.

Practice tools

How does prize card training work?
RdyDeck sets some cards from your deck aside as prizes, 6 by default or 4 or 8, and you flip through the rest. Then you name the cards that are missing. After a few games it shows which cards you miss most. The prize trainer page has more.
Can I goldfish my deck in RdyDeck?
Yes. Open a deck, tap the Hand tab, then Goldfish (3 Turns). You play your first three turns alone with real draws and the normal rules, and RdyDeck shows how your setup compares with the odds. How to goldfish a deck explains it step by step.
What are opening odds?
How often your deck mulligans, prizes a key card or misses its first attacker. RdyDeck works them out for your exact list. See opening odds.

Privacy and data

What happens to my decks?
They stay in your browser on your device. We do not have a copy. Clearing your browser's site data, or Settings and Clear all data in the app, deletes them.
Does RdyDeck track me?
RdyDeck has no accounts and keeps your data on your device. The privacy policy lists every outside service the site talks to and why.
