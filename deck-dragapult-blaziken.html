Dragapult Blaziken deck guide

A Dragapult ex build with Blaziken ex. Seething Spirit attaches a Basic Energy from your discard pile every turn, so Dragapult ex can attack again soon after it is Knocked Out.

  • Online record: 54.9% wins over 3,637 games since 16 Sep 2026, and 53.2% over 278 games since Delta Reign.
  • RdyDeck grade: 8.8 out of 10 for its top lists.
Grade my Dragapult Blaziken list

Game plan

The core is the same as Dragapult: Dreepy, Drakloak and Dragapult ex, with Buddy-Buddy Poffin to bench Dreepy and Drakloak's Recon Directive to draw on the way up. Phantom Dive costs Fire and Psychic, does 200 and puts 6 damage counters on your opponent's Benched Pokémon in any way you like.

Blaziken ex's Seething Spirit attaches a Basic Energy card from your discard pile to 1 of your Pokémon once a turn. The deck plays Fire, Psychic and Darkness Energy, so Blaziken ex can pay for Phantom Dive or give Munkidori the Darkness Energy its Adrena-Brain needs to move damage counters. Blaziken ex can attack too: Smolder-sault does 200 for Fire and one more Energy, though it can't attack during your next turn.

Rare Candy takes Torchic straight to Blaziken ex and Dreepy straight to Dragapult ex, skipping the Stage 1. Dawn finds a Basic, a Stage 1 and a Stage 2 Pokémon at once, and Crispin finds 2 Basic Energy of different types, attaching one and putting the other in your hand. Night Stretcher gets back a Pokémon or a Basic Energy from your discard pile.
