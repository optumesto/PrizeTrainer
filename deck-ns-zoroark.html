N's Zoroark Deck Guide

N's Zoroark ex copies attacks. Night Joker uses an attack from one of your Benched N's Pokémon for just two Darkness Energy, so the deck benches N's Zekrom and N's Reshiram and borrows their biggest hits.

  • Field share: 4% of players in the 5 Limitless online events in the 14 days to 23 Nov 2026 (1,014 players).
  • Tournament record: 50.4% wins over 6,415 games since 16 Sep 2026, and 52.3% over 1,251 games since Delta Reign.
  • RdyDeck grade: 8.1 out of 10 for its top lists.

Game Plan

Night Joker costs two Darkness Energy and lets N's Zoroark ex use any attack of one of your Benched N's Pokémon. N's Zekrom's Rampaging Thunder does 250 but needs Fire, two Lightning and Colorless Energy on Zekrom itself. Through Night Joker, Zoroark ex only pays its own two Darkness. Rampaging Thunder still stops the Pokémon that used it from attacking during your next turn, so an N's Zoroark ex that copies it has to retreat or sit out a turn. N's PP Up attaches a Basic Energy from your discard pile to a Benched N's Pokémon, and N's Castle gives every N's Pokémon in play no Retreat Cost.

Each N's Zoroark ex in play can use Trade once a turn: discard a card from your hand and draw 2. With two or three on the board, the deck draws deep every turn. Pecharunt ex's Subjugating Chains switches one of your Benched Darkness Pokémon into the Active Spot and makes it Poisoned, and Binding Mochi adds 40 damage to the attacks of the Poisoned Pokémon it is attached to. Black Belt's Training adds 40 more against a Pokémon ex that turn.

Put together, a Poisoned N's Zoroark ex with Binding Mochi copying Rampaging Thunder after Black Belt's Training does 330 damage to a Pokémon ex. Munkidori's Adrena-Brain moves damage counters onto your opponent's Pokémon, and Yveltal is a single prize attacker for when trading two prizes would lose the race.

How RdyDeck Grades It

RdyDeck grades the 6 N's Zoroark lists in its test set, all top 16 finishes from 8 Nov 2026 to 9 Nov 2026, at 8.1 out of 10 (the middle value). Their middle Synergy is 8.3 and their middle Power is 8.2. The grade is not simply Synergy times Power, since it also counts the deck's online results and lowers the grade for how tightly a list fits only when the fit is loose.

RdyDeck counts the copied Rampaging Thunder at 290 for Power, since the full 330 needs both Black Belt's Training and Binding Mochi. Synergy reads lower than most top decks because several lines run single copies, but that only lowers the grade when the fit is very loose. Its online record is about even.

Your own list will grade differently, since the grade reads every card you run. How Grading Works explains each part.
