Dragapult deck guide

Dragapult ex wins by spreading damage. Every Phantom Dive does 200 to the Active Pokémon and puts 6 more damage counters wherever you like on the Bench. Over the last two sets it has the best record of any deck with more than 2,000 games.

  • Field share: 8.5% of players in the 5 Limitless online events in the 14 days to 10 Nov 2026 (1,266 players).
  • Online record: 55.6% wins over 9,573 games since 16 Sep 2026, and 55.6% over 478 games since Delta Reign.
  • RdyDeck grade: 8.8 out of 10 for its top lists.

Game plan

Dreepy evolves into Drakloak and then Dragapult ex, a Stage 2 line that most lists play without Rare Candy. That sounds slow, but Drakloak's Recon Directive looks at the top 2 cards of your deck and puts 1 into your hand every turn, so each Drakloak is a draw engine on the way up. Buddy-Buddy Poffin puts two Basic Pokémon with 70 HP or less, like Dreepy, onto your Bench at once.

Phantom Dive costs only Fire and Psychic, does 200 damage, and puts 6 damage counters on your opponent's Benched Pokémon in any way you like. Munkidori's Adrena-Brain moves up to 3 damage counters from one of your Pokémon to one of your opponent's each turn while it has Darkness Energy attached, so damage Dragapult takes comes back out onto the other board. Spread damage sets up turns where one attack Knocks Out the Active Pokémon and a damaged Benched Pokémon together.

Dragapult ex is a Tera Pokémon, so damage from attacks can't hurt it while it is on your Bench. Budew's Itchy Pollen costs no Energy and stops your opponent playing Items from their hand during their next turn, which buys time while your Stage 2 Pokémon come online. Rosa's Encouragement attaches up to two Basic Energy from your discard pile to a Stage 2 Pokémon when you have more Prize cards left than your opponent, so a fresh Dragapult ex can attack right away after one is Knocked Out.

How RdyDeck grades it

RdyDeck grades the 13 Dragapult lists in its test set, all top 16 finishes from 7 Nov 2026 to 9 Nov 2026, at 8.8 out of 10 (the middle value). Their middle Synergy is 8.9 and their middle Power is 8.8. The grade is not simply Synergy times Power, since it also counts the deck's online results and lowers the grade for how tightly a list fits only when the fit is loose.

RdyDeck grades Dragapult as tournament ready. Power counts its spread as more than the 200 printed on Phantom Dive, Drakloak's draw keeps Setup speed high without Rare Candy, and most of the list gives up a single prize. Its strong online record adds to the grade.
