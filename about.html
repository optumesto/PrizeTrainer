About RdyDeck

RdyDeck is a free Pokémon TCG deck checker and practice tool. Paste a decklist and it tells you how ready the deck is, what to fix first, and how decks like it are doing in real tournaments.

Why it exists

Most players test a new list by playing it, losing a few games and guessing why. RdyDeck gives a second opinion before that: it reads every card's text, checks that the cards support each other and that the plan can win, and compares the deck with what is winning online right now. Then it gives a plain grade out of 10 and a short list of what to change.

The goal is a grade that matches real results. A list that tops events should grade well, and a list with a real gap should show it. Every change to the grader is checked against a fixed set of recent top 16 lists, so improving one deck does not quietly break another.

What it does

  • Deck checker: a grade out of 10, with Synergy, Power and tournament results explained in plain sentences. How grading works covers the details.
  • Deck builder: the core cards and common flex slots that top lists run for each deck, with counts, so you can see what your list is missing.
  • Against the meta: how decks like yours do against each of the most played decks.
  • Opening odds: how often your exact list mulligans, prizes a key card or misses its first attacker.
  • Rotation checker: which cards are not Standard legal now and which may rotate next.
  • Prize trainer: drills that teach you to work out your prize cards quickly.
  • Deck guides: the top decks right now, with core lists, game plans and matchups.

Who makes it

RdyDeck is made and run by Optume Ventures LLC, a Pennsylvania company. Much of what changes in it comes from players: bug reports sent from the app, and comments on the RdyDeck videos on YouTube.

Follow RdyDeck

RdyDeck's official account is @rdydeck on Instagram, with deck breakdowns.

Free, and staying free

Every feature is free, there is no account and nothing is locked behind a paid tier. The website is planned to be paid for by a few ads on menu and information screens, never during a game or drill, and never as pop-ups. Your decks stay on your own device, as the privacy policy explains.

Where the data comes from

  • Card data: TCGdex, a free and open card database, with the Pokémon TCG API as a backup.
  • Tournament results and decklists: Limitless TCG online tournaments, read once a week and credited wherever they appear.
  • Fonts: Saira and Saira Condensed by Omnibus-Type, under the SIL Open Font License.
