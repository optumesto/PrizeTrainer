Pokémon TCG rules, from the official rulebook

Common Pokémon TCG rules questions, answered from the official rulebook, tournament handbook and rules posts that The Pokémon Company International publishes. Each answer is in our own words and names the document and the page or section it comes from, so you can check it there. RdyDeck shows the matching rules when you tap a card in a deck.

Setup

How do you set up a game?
Flip a coin, and the winner decides who goes first. Each player shuffles their 60 card deck and draws 7. Put one Basic Pokémon face down as your Active Pokémon and up to 5 more face down on your Bench. Set the top 6 cards of your deck aside face down as your Prize cards, then both players turn their Pokémon face up and the game starts.
Source: Pokémon TCG Rulebook, The Pokémon Company International (Twilight Masquerade edition, 2024, p. 8). Checked 16 Nov 2026.
What happens if my opening hand has no Basic Pokémon?
That is a mulligan. Show your hand to your opponent, shuffle it back into your deck and draw 7 again, until you have a Basic Pokémon. If both players have none, both show their hands and start over. Otherwise your opponent finishes setting up first, then may draw 1 card for each extra mulligan you took, and may put any Basic Pokémon drawn onto their Bench.
Source: Pokémon TCG Rulebook, The Pokémon Company International (Twilight Masquerade edition, 2024, pp. 8 and 18). Checked 16 Nov 2026.

Damage

In what order is an attack's damage worked out?
Start from the damage printed on the attack. Apply effects on the attacking Pokémon, then add Weakness, then take away Resistance, then apply effects on the Pokémon being hit. Put 1 damage counter on it for each 10 damage left.
Source: Pokémon TCG Rulebook, The Pokémon Company International (Twilight Masquerade edition, 2024, p. 20). Checked 16 Nov 2026.
What if the damage works out to 0?
If the damage ends at 0 or less, no damage counters are put on the Pokémon. If the attack's own damage is 0, the other damage steps are skipped.
Source: Pokémon TCG Rulebook, The Pokémon Company International (Twilight Masquerade edition, 2024, p. 20). Checked 16 Nov 2026.
Do Weakness and Resistance change an attack that puts damage counters on a Pokémon?
No. Putting damage counters on a Pokémon is not doing damage, so Weakness, Resistance and effects that change damage do not apply. You put on exactly the counters the attack says. Each counter stands for 10 damage.
Source: Pokémon TCG Rulebook, The Pokémon Company International (Twilight Masquerade edition, 2024, pp. 14 and 20). Checked 16 Nov 2026.
Do Weakness and Resistance count when an attack hits the Bench?
No. Weakness and Resistance only count for the Active Pokémon. Damage to a Benched Pokémon ignores both.
Source: Pokémon TCG Rulebook, The Pokémon Company International (Twilight Masquerade edition, 2024, p. 14). Checked 16 Nov 2026.
What happens to an attack's effect when the Pokémon moves to the Bench?
Effects of attacks on the Active Pokémon end when it goes to the Bench. Its damage counters stay on it.
Source: Pokémon TCG Rulebook, The Pokémon Company International (Twilight Masquerade edition, 2024, pp. 12 and 20). Checked 16 Nov 2026.

Pokémon ex

What does Tera do on the Bench?
While a Tera Pokémon ex is on the Bench, attacks do no damage to it, whether they are your opponent's attacks or your own. When it is Knocked Out, your opponent still takes 2 Prize cards.
Source: Pokémon TCG Rulebook, The Pokémon Company International (Twilight Masquerade edition, 2024, p. 25). Checked 16 Nov 2026.
How many Prize cards is a Pokémon ex worth?
When a Pokémon ex is Knocked Out, its owner's opponent takes 2 Prize cards instead of 1. This rulebook edition came out before Mega Evolution Pokémon ex, which carry their own rule on the card: 3 Prize cards.
Source: Pokémon TCG Rulebook, The Pokémon Company International (Twilight Masquerade edition, 2024, p. 24). Checked 16 Nov 2026.

Deck building

How many ACE SPEC cards can a deck have?
One. A deck can hold only 1 ACE SPEC card in total, not 1 of each.
Source: Pokémon TCG Rulebook, The Pokémon Company International (Twilight Masquerade edition, 2024, p. 23). Checked 16 Nov 2026.
Which cards are legal in Standard?
Cards with regulation mark H, I or J. Cards with the G mark are Expanded only.
Source: Play! Pokémon TCG Tournament Handbook, The Pokémon Company International (revised 21 May 2026, section 4.1.1). Checked 16 Nov 2026.

Turns

What can't the player going first do on their first turn?
They cannot attack and cannot play a Supporter on that first turn.
Source: Pokémon TCG Rulebook, The Pokémon Company International (Twilight Masquerade edition, 2024, pp. 12 and 13). Checked 16 Nov 2026.
How many Supporters can you play in a turn?
One per turn.
Source: Pokémon TCG Rulebook, The Pokémon Company International (Twilight Masquerade edition, 2024, p. 12). Checked 16 Nov 2026.
How often can you retreat?
Once per turn. Discard Energy from the Active Pokémon equal to its Retreat Cost, then switch it with a Benched Pokémon. You can still attack that turn.
Source: Pokémon TCG Rulebook, The Pokémon Company International (Twilight Masquerade edition, 2024, p. 12). Checked 16 Nov 2026.
When a card says "up to" a number, can you choose 0?
For an attack, yes: you can choose any number from 0 to the most it allows. For other effects, like Trainer cards and Abilities, you must choose at least 1, unless nothing can be chosen. An effect that searches for "any" card, without naming a kind, also needs at least 1.
Source: February 2026 Rule Book Updates, The Pokémon Company International (Pokémon Professor Community post, 26 Feb 2026, "Up to" change). Checked 16 Nov 2026.
Who decides the order when several effects happen at the same time?
During a turn, the player whose turn it is chooses the order. During Pokémon Checkup, the player who takes the next turn chooses. A card's own effects all finish before any other effect starts.
Source: February 2026 Rule Book Updates, The Pokémon Company International (Pokémon Professor Community post, 26 Feb 2026, ordering rules). Checked 16 Nov 2026.

Evolution

When can a Pokémon evolve?
Not on the turn it came into play, and not on either player's own first turn, unless a card says otherwise. The same Pokémon cannot evolve twice in one turn. Evolving keeps its damage counters and attached cards, and ends Special Conditions and effects of attacks on it.
Source: Pokémon TCG Rulebook, The Pokémon Company International (Twilight Masquerade edition, 2024, p. 11). Checked 16 Nov 2026.

Special Conditions

How do Special Conditions end?
The five Special Conditions are Asleep, Burned, Confused, Paralyzed and Poisoned, and only the Active Pokémon can have them. They all end when it moves to the Bench or evolves. An Asleep or Paralyzed Pokémon cannot retreat.
Source: Pokémon TCG Rulebook, The Pokémon Company International (Twilight Masquerade edition, 2024, pp. 15 and 16). Checked 16 Nov 2026.
In what order does Pokémon Checkup go?
Poisoned, then Burned, then Asleep, then Paralyzed. Other effects that happen during Checkup come before or after those, never in between, and Knocked Out Pokémon are handled at the end.
Source: Pokémon TCG Rulebook, The Pokémon Company International (Twilight Masquerade edition, 2024, p. 15). Checked 16 Nov 2026.

Trainers

How many Pokémon Tools can a Pokémon have?
One at a time. You can play as many Tools in a turn as you like.
Source: Pokémon TCG Rulebook, The Pokémon Company International (Twilight Masquerade edition, 2024, p. 41). Checked 16 Nov 2026.
How do Stadiums work?
You can play 1 Stadium per turn. A new Stadium replaces the one in play, and you cannot play a Stadium with the same name as the one already in play.
Source: Pokémon TCG Rulebook, The Pokémon Company International (Twilight Masquerade edition, 2024, p. 12). Checked 16 Nov 2026.

Abilities

Can you use an Ability from the Bench?
Yes. Abilities work from the Active Spot or the Bench, and you can still attack after using one.
Source: Pokémon TCG Rulebook, The Pokémon Company International (Twilight Masquerade edition, 2024, pp. 12 and 40). Checked 16 Nov 2026.

Winning

What if both players win at the same time?
Since the February 2026 update, the rulebook no longer uses Sudden Death. If one player meets more win conditions than the other, that player wins. Otherwise you play a tiebreaker game with the usual 6 Prize cards. Play! Pokémon tournaments already worked this way.
Source: February 2026 Rule Book Updates, The Pokémon Company International (Pokémon Professor Community post, 26 Feb 2026, Sudden Death change). Checked 16 Nov 2026.

Card rulings

How does Mega Zygarde ex's Nullifying Zero work against a Benched Tera Pokémon?
You flip a coin for each of your opponent's Pokémon in play, in any order, and each heads puts 150 damage on that Pokémon. You still flip for a Benched Tera Pokémon, but it takes no damage while it is on the Bench.
Source: ME: Perfect Order FAQ, The Pokémon Company International (Pokémon Professor Community post, 13 Mar 2026, Pokémon Attacks section, entry ME3_47), PDF. Checked 16 Nov 2026.

What is not here yet

Pokémon also rules on how two specific cards work together. Most of those rulings are not on this page yet: RdyDeck only adds one when The Pokémon Company International has published it where anyone can read it. When a rule here and a card's text disagree, the card's text wins, and a judge at your event has the final word.
