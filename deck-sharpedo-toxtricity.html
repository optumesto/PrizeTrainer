Sharpedo Toxtricity deck guide

Mega Sharpedo ex hits harder once it is damaged: Hungry Jaws does 120, or 270 if Sharpedo has any damage counters on it. Toxtricity puts those damage counters on it on purpose.

  • Online record: 50.3% wins over 3,597 games since 16 Sep 2026, and 46.0% over 420 games since Delta Reign.
  • RdyDeck grade: 8.2 out of 10 for its top lists.
Grade my Sharpedo Toxtricity list

Game plan

Toxtricity's Sinister Surge searches your deck for a Basic Darkness Energy, attaches it to 1 of your Benched Darkness Pokémon and places 2 damage counters on that Pokémon. Used on a Benched Mega Sharpedo ex, one Ability both powers it up and turns on Hungry Jaws' extra 150 damage. Each Toxtricity in play can use it once a turn, and Toxel's Call for Family benches 2 Basic Pokémon to start.

Pecharunt ex's Subjugating Chains switches one of your Benched Darkness Pokémon, other than a Pecharunt ex, into the Active Spot and makes it Poisoned. Here that helps: Binding Mochi gives the attacks of the Poisoned Pokémon it is attached to 40 more damage against your opponent's Active Pokémon, and Poison adds damage counters between turns, which keeps Hungry Jaws switched on. Mega Sharpedo ex has 330 HP to spare, but it gives up 3 Prize cards when Knocked Out.

Munkidori's Adrena-Brain moves up to 3 damage counters from one of your Pokémon to one of your opponent's while it has Darkness Energy attached, so damage Sharpedo takes can be passed on. Tatsugiri's Attract Customers finds a Supporter from the top 6 cards while it is in the Active Spot, and Sharpedo's own Greedy Fang does 70 and draws 2 cards for one Energy.
