Ogerpon Meganium Hydrapple deck guide

This Grass deck puts as many Basic Grass Energy into play as it can. Meganium's Wild Growth makes each one provide two Grass Energy, and Hydrapple ex's Syrup Storm grows with every Grass Energy on your board.

  • Field share: 3.9% of players in the 5 Limitless online events in the 14 days to 15 Nov 2026 (1,007 players).
  • Online record: 53.9% wins over 4,238 games since 16 Sep 2026, and 54.6% over 676 games since Delta Reign.
  • RdyDeck grade: 8.8 out of 10 for its top lists.

Game plan

Teal Mask Ogerpon ex is the early attacker and an engine: Teal Dance attaches a Basic Grass Energy from your hand to it once a turn and draws a card. Its Myriad Leaf Shower does 30 plus 30 for each Energy attached to both Active Pokémon. Hydrapple ex's Ripening Charge attaches another Basic Grass Energy from your hand to one of your Pokémon each turn and heals 30 damage from it.

Meganium and Hydrapple ex are Stage 2 Pokémon, and the list plays no Rare Candy. Forest of Vitality lets Grass Pokémon evolve during the turn they are played, except on a player's first turn, which saves time on both lines. Bug Catching Set looks at the top 7 cards of your deck and takes up to 2 Grass Pokémon or Basic Grass Energy, and Celebi's Traverse Time searches for up to 3 Grass Pokémon and Stadiums for one Grass Energy.

Once Meganium is in play, Wild Growth makes each Basic Grass Energy on your Pokémon provide two Grass Energy. Syrup Storm costs two Colorless and does 30 plus 30 for each Grass Energy attached to all of your Pokémon, so five Basic Grass Energy cards in play make 330 damage. Lana's Aid brings back Pokémon without a Rule Box and Basic Energy from your discard pile for the long game.

How RdyDeck grades it

RdyDeck grades the 5 Ogerpon Meganium Hydrapple lists in its test set, all top 16 finishes from 8 Nov 2026 to 9 Nov 2026, at 8.8 out of 10 (the middle value). Their middle Synergy is 8.1 and their middle Power is 8.8. The grade is not simply Synergy times Power, since it also counts the deck's online results and lowers the grade for how tightly a list fits only when the fit is loose.

Damage is full marks for this deck, since Syrup Storm can reach 330 with five Grass Energy cards in play. Setup speed is where it loses points: both Stage 2 lines evolve the long way without Rare Candy, so RdyDeck expects them to attack from your third turn. The Setup speed check does not count Forest of Vitality yet, so it may undersell how fast real games go.
