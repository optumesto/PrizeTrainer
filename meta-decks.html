Reading the numbers

A win rate a little over 50% is good in a format this crowded: most decks sit between 47% and 53%, and only a handful stay above 55% over thousands of games. A deck with a small share and a high win rate can be a strong pick that few people have found yet, or a deck that has only played a few events, so check the number of games before reading much into it.

The grade and the record measure different things. The grade reads the cards in a list: do they work together, and can the plan win. The record shows how the deck has actually done. RdyDeck blends the two, and How grading works explains how much each one counts.
