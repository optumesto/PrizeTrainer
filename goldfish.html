How to goldfish a Pokémon deck

Goldfishing means playing your deck on your own, with no opponent, to see how it draws and how fast it sets up. The name comes from other card games: a goldfish never plays back. RdyDeck lets you goldfish your own Pokémon TCG list for free on your phone or computer. It deals an opening hand, sets aside 6 Prize cards and lets you play your first three turns.

Goldfish my deck

What goldfishing tells you

One hand tells you very little. Ten or twenty hands show you how the deck really behaves. Goldfishing answers four questions about a list:

  • Opening hands: how often you mulligan, and how often you start with a Basic Pokémon you are happy to have Active.
  • Setup speed: whether your main attacker is in play, evolved and powered up by your second or third turn.
  • Prized cards: how often a card you only run one or two of ends up in your Prize cards, and whether the deck still works when it does.
  • Card counts: whether you run enough draw Supporters, search cards and Energy, or too many of one and too few of another.

How to goldfish a deck with real cards

  1. Shuffle your 60 cards and draw 7.
  2. If the 7 have no Basic Pokémon, that is a mulligan: shuffle them back and draw 7 again. Count your mulligans.
  3. Put a Basic Pokémon in the Active Spot and any others you want on your Bench, up to 5.
  4. Set the top 6 cards aside face down as your Prize cards.
  5. Decide whether you are going first or second. Going first, you cannot play a Supporter or attack on your first turn.
  6. Play each turn as you would in a game: draw a card, attach one Energy, play one Supporter at most and as many Items as you like. A Pokémon cannot evolve on your first turn or on the turn it was played.
  7. The first time a card lets you search your deck, look through it and work out which cards are missing. Those are your Prize cards. Write them down, then check them at the end.
  8. After each turn, note what is in play and what you still need. Once your setup is done or clearly stuck, write down the turn it happened on and start again with a fresh shuffle.

These are the setup, mulligan and first turn rules from the official Pokémon TCG rulebook (pages 8, 12 and 13). The rules page has them with their sources.

How to goldfish in RdyDeck

  1. Open RdyDeck. Paste a list exported from Pokémon TCG Live or copied from Limitless TCG, import it from a photo, or pick one of the built in starter decks.
  2. Open the deck and tap the Hand tab.
  3. Turn 1 line deals a random opening 7, redealing hands with no Basic and counting the mulligans. Pick the Pokémon you would build around and RdyDeck shows how often that line comes together with this hand.
  4. Switch to Goldfish (3 Turns), or tap a Basic in your opening hand to carry that same hand into Goldfish.
  5. Choose going first or second, put your Basics in play and start turn 1. Tap a card in your hand to see what it can do now and play it, tap a Pokémon in play to use its Ability or retreat, and attack with your Active Pokémon once its Energy pays the cost. RdyDeck draws for you each turn and keeps the rules: one Energy and one Supporter a turn, no Supporter on turn 1 going first, and no evolving on your first turn or onto a Pokémon that came in this turn.
  6. After turn 3 you see your board, what came together on which turn, and how that compares with the odds for the hand you were dealt.

Abilities, Items, Supporters and Stadiums do what their card text says. Damage counters stay on your Pokémon with their HP left, so self damage, Risky Ruins and healing cards like Pokémon Center Lady all count, and a Pokémon whose damage reaches its HP is Knocked Out. When RdyDeck cannot follow a card yet, it shows the card's text and you finish it yourself with the Draw, Search, Discard and damage counter buttons.

There is no opponent. An attack's effects on your own side happen and then your turn ends, but its damage to the opponent is only noted in the log, and nobody takes Prize cards. When one of your Pokémon is Knocked Out, the log says how many Prize cards your opponent would take.

Practice the parts of a game goldfishing misses

Goldfishing shows how your deck sets up when nothing gets in its way. Real games have hand disruption like Iono and Judge, attacks aimed at your Bench and an opponent racing you, so test against a friend or a second deck too. RdyDeck has other free tools for the parts you can practice alone:

  • Opening odds: the Odds tab works out how often your exact list mulligans, prizes a key card or misses its first attacker, without dealing hands one by one.
  • Prize trainer: tap Prizes on a deck in your list, or open the deck's Drills tab and tap Play under Prize game. RdyDeck sets Prize cards aside, 6 by default or 4 or 8, and you flip through the rest and name what is missing. You can add a time limit, and after a few games Progress shows the cards you miss most. The daily challenge gives everyone the same shuffle.
  • Drills: the tab after Hand on every deck. Besides the Prize game, Resource count asks how many of a card are left in your deck, and Opponent prize read asks you to work out an opponent's Prize cards from what they have shown.
  • Deck checker: grades the whole list out of 10 before you start testing it.

Questions about goldfishing

What does goldfishing mean in the Pokémon TCG?
Goldfishing is playing your deck alone, with no opponent, to test how it draws and how quickly it sets up. Players use it to check opening hands, Prize cards and card counts before a tournament.
How many turns should I goldfish?
Most decks show what they can do in the first two or three turns, so that is where goldfishing tells you the most. RdyDeck's Goldfish plays your first three turns, where most decks show what they can do.
How many hands should I goldfish?
Enough to see a pattern, ten or twenty at least. One good or bad hand is luck. For the exact chance of a mulligan or a prized card, use opening odds instead of counting hands.
Can I goldfish a Pokémon deck online for free?
Yes. RdyDeck is free, has no account and runs in your browser on a phone or computer. Your decks stay on your own device, and it works offline once it has loaded.
Where is Goldfish in RdyDeck?
Open a deck, tap the Hand tab, then Goldfish (3 Turns). You can also tap a Basic in a Turn 1 line hand to play that hand in Goldfish.
Does RdyDeck's Goldfish play against an opponent?
No. It is a solo test: you play your own first three turns with real draws and the normal rules. You can attack, and the attack's effects on your side happen, but there is no opponent to take damage and nobody takes Prize cards. For full games against another player, use Pokémon TCG Live or play in person.
Can I goldfish a deck from Pokémon TCG Live or Limitless?
Yes. Paste the export from Pokémon TCG Live or a list from Limitless TCG, one card per line with the count, name, set code and number, and RdyDeck reads it.
