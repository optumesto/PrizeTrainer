How to build your first Pokémon TCG deck

A Pokémon TCG deck has exactly 60 cards, no more than 4 cards with the same name except Basic Energy, and at least 1 Basic Pokémon. Inside those rules, a good first deck is built around one main attacker, the Pokémon and Energy it needs, and the draw and search cards nearly every winning deck plays.

The deck building rules

  • Exactly 60 cards. Not 59 and not 61.
  • No more than 4 cards with the same name, except Basic Energy, which has no limit. Cards with the same name count together even if their attacks are different.
  • At least 1 Basic Pokémon, since you need one to start the game. Your opening hand needs one too, or you mulligan.
  • Only 1 ACE SPEC card in the whole deck, not 1 of each.

Source: Pokémon TCG Rulebook, The Pokémon Company International (Twilight Masquerade edition, 2024, pp. 22 and 23).

For tournaments, every card also has to be legal in the format you play. In Standard that is set by each card's regulation mark, and the rotation checker marks which of your cards are legal now. The rules page answers more rules questions, each with its source.

Start from a deck that already works

The rulebook's own advice is to take a ready-made ex Battle Deck you like and change it, keeping it at exactly 60 cards. You can do the same with a winning list: the deck guides show the cards top players run in each deck, and RdyDeck has starter decks built in, so you can open one and change it card by card.

Pick one attacker and one or two Energy types

Choose the Pokémon you want to attack with, then put in its whole evolution line and the Energy its attack costs. The rulebook suggests one or two Energy types at most, and 92 of the 132 top 16 lists we test with play two types of Basic Energy or fewer. A second attacker for matchups where the first one struggles is the next thing to add.

How many Pokémon, Trainers and Energy

The rulebook's starting point is 12 to 15 Energy and about 20 to 25 Trainer cards, with the rest Pokémon. Winning lists today lean further toward Trainers. Across 132 recent top 16 lists from Limitless online tournaments, the typical deck ran:

Common questions

How many cards are in a Pokémon TCG deck?
Exactly 60. The deck can have no more than 4 cards with the same name, except Basic Energy, and needs at least 1 Basic Pokémon.
How many Energy should a beginner Pokémon deck have?
The official rulebook suggests 12 to 15 Energy in a new 60 card deck. Recent top 16 lists run fewer, 9 in a typical list, since many of them use Trainers and Abilities that find or attach Energy.
Can I put more than 4 of a card in my deck?
Only Basic Energy. Every other card is limited to 4 copies with the same name, and ACE SPEC cards to 1 in the whole deck.
Is there a free tool to check my first deck?
Yes. RdyDeck rates a 60 card deck out of 10 for free and lists what to fix first. It checks how the deck plays, not the condition of your cards.
