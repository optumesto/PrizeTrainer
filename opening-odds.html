Mulligan odds by Basic count

What RdyDeck adds for your list

  • Mulligan: the chance your opening 7 has no Basic.
  • Draw card on turn 1: the chance of a draw card you can use in your first 8 cards.
  • Attacker by your second turn: how often your main attacker is ready, from raw draws and with search. For a Stage 2 with no Rare Candy in the list, the app counts to your third turn instead, the earliest it can be in play.
  • Prized pieces: how often every copy of something your attacker needs ends up in the prizes.

Mulligan and prize odds are exact. The setup odds come from 20,000 simulated shuffles of your exact list. Search cards and draw Supporters are not played out, so setup odds are floors, not forecasts. The shuffle is seeded from the list, so the same list always shows the same numbers.
