Slowking deck guide

Slowking borrows other Pokémon's attacks. Its Seek Inspiration discards the top card of your deck and, if that card is a Pokémon without a Rule Box, uses one of its attacks. The deck sets up the top of its own deck so it knows what Slowking will copy.

  • Field share: 5.2% of players in the 5 Limitless online events in the 14 days to 10 Nov 2026 (1,266 players).
  • Online record: 51.5% wins over 4,332 games since 16 Sep 2026, and 49.2% over 303 games since Delta Reign.
  • RdyDeck grade: 8.5 out of 10 for its top lists.

Game plan

Seek Inspiration costs Psychic and Colorless. Academy at Night lets each player put a card from their hand on top of their deck once during their turn, and Ciphermaniac's Codebreaking searches your deck for any 2 cards and puts them on top in the order you choose. Either way, you decide which Pokémon Seek Inspiration discards.

Kyurem is the usual target. Its Trifrost does 110 damage to 3 of your opponent's Pokémon, so two uses through Slowking can Knock Out three Pokémon with 220 HP or less. Trifrost also discards all Energy from the attacking Pokémon. Boomerang Energy helps here: when it is discarded by an effect of an attack used by the Pokémon it is attached to, it is attached back to that Pokémon after the attack. Annihilape's Ghostly Blow and Zeraora's Thunder Raid give Slowking other options in some lists.

Telepathic Psychic Energy searches your deck for up to 2 Basic Psychic Pokémon and puts them onto your Bench when you attach it from your hand to a Psychic Pokémon, which fills the board with Slowpoke on the first turns. Wondrous Patch attaches a Basic Psychic Energy from your discard pile to a Benched Psychic Pokémon. Mega Kangaskhan ex draws 2 a turn from the Active Spot, and Latias ex keeps retreat free for your Basic Pokémon.

How RdyDeck grades it

RdyDeck grades the 5 Slowking lists in its test set, all top 16 finishes from 7 Nov 2026 to 9 Nov 2026, at 8.5 out of 10 (the middle value). Their middle Synergy is 9.0 and their middle Power is 8.8. The grade is not simply Synergy times Power, since it also counts the deck's online results and lowers the grade for how tightly a list fits only when the fit is loose.

RdyDeck reads the Kyurem copy and counts Slowking as hitting three Pokémon at once. Its main caution for most Slowking lists is that they run no Boss's Orders or Prime Catcher, so the deck can only Knock Out what your opponent puts in the Active Spot, which Trifrost's Bench damage partly makes up for.

Your own list will grade differently, since the grade reads every card you run. How grading works explains each part.
